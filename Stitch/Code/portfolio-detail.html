Project 007 / 2024

UNIT 7
RECON

Role

Lead Character Artist

The
Craft

An exploration of silent lethality. Unit 7 Recon was designed as the ultimate synthesis of biomechanical engineering and clandestine aesthetics. The vision was to create a silhouette that feels both monolithic and agile, drawing inspiration from predatory anatomy and brutalist architecture.

Complexity

142K TRIS

Texture Sets

5x 4K PBR

Workflow

UDIM / ACES

Software

ZBrush Maya Substance

Technical Breakdown

Phase 01

Digital Sculpting

High-fidelity sculpting performed in ZBrush. Emphasis on anatomical accuracy beneath the hard-surface components to ensure realistic deformation during animation. Every micro-scratch and panel line was manually carved to achieve a handcrafted artisanal feel.

Phase 02

Topology & UVs

Retopology focused on edge-flow for cinematic rigging. The mesh is optimized for sub-division surfaces while maintaining a manageable base-poly count. UV layout utilizes 5 UDIM tiles to maximize texel density on critical areas like the optics and primary armor plates.

Phase 03

PBR Texturing

Material definition via Substance Painter. We utilized a layered shader approach: a base obsidian ceramic, topped with a metallic gold leaf, finished with a procedural dust and wear layer. The Roughness map was key to defining the 'premium' tactile feel of the suit.
